Includes a short warm-up (30 seconds unless you change it; 0 skips it) and a 2-minute cool-down. Not a medical device. Stop if you feel unwell.
How to play: punch blue orbs with your left hand and orange with your right. Orbs with a white curve: swing along the curve (uppercut or hook) for double points. Low orbs: squat and punch. Purple walls: get your head into the open gap (duck, lean, squat). If your heart rate stalls, the game adds challenge and leans on whatever raises it most for you. The gauge on the left freezes when you're below your zone and burns when you're in it.
Quest: press Enter VR below. Y or B opens the options board on your left (point with the laser, pull the trigger). A/X restarts after a workout.
