## New moves
- **Finishing slam** after battle ropes and two-hand ropes: smash a gold bar with both fists.
- **Side shifts** (below the zone only): step onto a floor pad 35 cm left or right.
- **Boxing combos on the music beat**, with a finisher and a cooldown so they don't take over.
- **Heavy bag** burst: called left hook / right hook / uppercut / power patches, with a 0.3 s white flash before each new target.
- **Sled run** burst: tuck into a squat and double-pole with both fists.
- **Dodge barrage**: bombs follow your head, whistle from their side when they lock, then you have 0.7 s to lean, duck or step.
- **Narrower punch orbs** (less reaching).

## Heart-rate control
- **CLIMBING / SETTLING** cue: shows when your effort is already enough to reach the zone, before your heart catches up (bias-free projection).
- **Workout shapes**: Steady, Waves (±5 BPM), Build & peak.
- **Spotlight rounds**: 60 s featuring one of your top heart-raising moves.
- **Breathing pacer** above the zone (the reactor breathes 4 s in, 6 s out).
- Challenge boost never builds while CLIMBING, while the phone is asleep or while the sensor is stuck.
- The model only learns from real phone heart rate and verified reps; faster early learning; stuck-sensor guard.

## Rewards, feedback and HUD
- Zone streaks (bell chord every 15 s in the zone).
- End-of-workout board: heart-rate graph, time in zone, personal bests, top 3 heart-raising moves.
- Tiered call-outs (safety first, praise floats at the orb), a decluttered HUD, first-time ghost-fist demos, a drift guard that points you back to centre.
- Pooled shard effects for Quest frame rate.

## Music
- Song tone follows your zone (brighter and more bass in the zone), ducking under big hits.
- Song breakdowns (in the zone only) that slam back in on your next big hit.

## Test tools (Options → Test → Diag)
- Diagnostics line on the scoreboard, Test buttons for every move, in-VR Note bookmarks, a test report on the end board, extra CSV columns (`reason`, `effort_bpm`, `trend`, `note`).

## Reviews
Four independent code reviews (10:11, 10:59, 13:05, 14:00) plus a final check at 14:15; every confirmed bug was fixed. A sweep of six settings combinations ran with 0 overlaps and 0 errors. Nothing has been tested in the headset yet.
