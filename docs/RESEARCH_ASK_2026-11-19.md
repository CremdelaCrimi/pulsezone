Hi! New research task for PulseZone (heart-rate-adaptive VR workout, Meta Quest WebXR, now on v56: https://cremdelacrimi.github.io/pulsezone/).

**Task: research the top VR workout/fitness games and compare them with PulseZone. Tell us what they have that we don't, and which of those ideas are worth building.**

Games to cover at least: Supernatural, FitXR, Les Mills Bodycombat, Thrill of the Fight 1 & 2, Beat Saber (fitness use), Synth Riders, Pistol Whip, OhShape, Audio Trip, Litesport (Liteboxer), VZfit, HoloFit, Dance Central / Just Dance VR, Eleven Table Tennis (as a cardio outlier), Hot Squat / Hot Dogs. Add any others that matter, including anything that already uses a live heart rate (Supernatural's Apple Watch zones, FitXR's Watch link, Quest Move, YUR.fit, HoloFit HR).

For each game, be concrete about: the core movement vocabulary (what the body actually does), how sessions are structured (classes, programs, length, warm-up/cool-down), coaching (voice, demo avatars, form cues), music and licensing approach, progression and retention systems (streaks, weekly plans, levels, challenges, leaderboards, friends, badges), onboarding and setup friction, how (if at all) they use heart rate, what players praise, and what players complain about in reviews.

**What PulseZone already has** (so you can skip it): live heart rate from a Fitbit Charge 6 via an iPhone bridge; a PID controller that holds heart rate in a target zone by changing speed, spawn rate and which moves appear; an online learner that works out which moves raise THIS player's heart rate; moves = orbs (jab, hook, uppercut, squat punch, power orb, combos, hammer fists), rope tracing (one and two hand), walls (duck, lean, squat, stand tall, slalom), dodge missiles, bursts (speed bag, heavy bag, battle ropes, sled run), whole-body stations with a ghost coach (knee drive, wall ball, woodchopper, tree chop, tiptoe reach), wall-ball rounds on a tempo ladder, charge & coast intervals with a landing verdict, spotlight rounds, zone streaks, reactor meter, in-zone world lighting, breathing pacer above the zone, one song time-stretched to pace plus a synth mode, end-of-workout HR graph, personal bests, workout history (last 12), acts within a session, a weekly plan, warm-up/cool-down, Steady/Waves/Build workout shapes, safety ceiling by age. Hard limits: the Quest tracks head + two hands only (no legs), the player must stay on the spot (no stepping sideways/forward/back), and the whole thing is a web page in the Quest browser (no app store, simple geometry, no licensed music budget).

**Deliverable, in this order:**
1. A comparison table: feature/mechanic × game (and a PulseZone column marked have / partial / missing).
2. The 10 gaps that matter most, ranked by expected effect on (a) keeping a player coming back 3× a week and (b) heart-rate output, each with a one-paragraph design sketch adapted to our limits (head + hands, stay on the spot, web page, live heart rate as the unique hook).
3. Anything the other games do that we should deliberately NOT copy, and why.

Please cite where a claim comes from (store pages, reviews, articles, dev talks) so we can check it. Thank you!
