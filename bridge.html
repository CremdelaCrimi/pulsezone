Lock the phone into this page (recommended)

iPhone's Guided Access keeps Bluefy open with the screen on, so your heart rate never cuts out.

Set up once:

  1. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn it on.
  2. Passcode Settings → set a passcode (or use Face ID).
  3. Display Auto-Lock → Never.

Each workout: connect your Charge 6 here, then triple-click the side button and tap Start. To finish, triple-click again, enter your passcode and tap End.

Turn off Low Power Mode first, because it forces the screen to lock after 30 seconds. Dimming the screen is fine.

Tips
  • Charge 6: swipe down from the clock face and tap Broadcast HR (or use the exercise menu during a workout). Then tap Connect Charge 6 here, pick it, and on the tracker tap Share, then Start. It can share with only one app or machine at a time.
  • Keep this page open with the screen on (iPhone pauses web pages when locked or when you switch apps). Guided Access above is the easiest way.
  • The phone and headset connect directly to each other, so the heart-rate readings go straight from the phone to the game.
